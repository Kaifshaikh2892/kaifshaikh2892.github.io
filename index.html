✨ What is flm?

flm is a revolutionary desktop application that lets you train and converse with an artificial intelligence unlike any other. It uses a frozen language model — a powerful, pre-trained neural network — and couples it with the complete MaleCNS fly connectome. This means the AI doesn't just generate random text; its structure is influenced by the full map of a fruit fly's brain connections. The result is a chat experience that's both intellectually fascinating and surprisingly organic.

Whether you're a sci-fi enthusiast, a curious student, or a tech hobbyist, flm offers a delightful way to explore conversational AI right from your own computer. You don't need to write a single line of code. Just download, run, and start talking to a brain.

The magic happens entirely on your machine. No cloud, no subscription — just an offline, private, and deeply intriguing tool that puts a new kind of intelligence at your fingertips.

🖥️ System Requirements

To run flm without any hiccups, make sure your computer meets these minimum specs:

  • Processor: 2.0 GHz dual-core or better
  • RAM: At least 4 GB (8 GB recommended for smooth training)
  • Storage: 1 GB of free disk space
  • Graphics: Integrated GPU is fine, no dedicated card needed

These are typical requirements. If your computer is a few years old, it will likely still work perfectly.

🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: The app won't open. What should I do?
Ensure you have Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Right-click the app and select "Run as administrator". If that fails, verify your antivirus isn't blocking it.

Q: Can I use flm on Mac?
Currently, flm is designed for Windows only. Mac support may arrive in a future update.

Q: Is my data safe?
Absolutely. flm runs offline, so nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Q: How do I know if my computer can run it?
Check the System Requirements above. If your PC is newer than 2018, you're almost certainly fine.

Q: I get a "missing DLL" error.
Download the latest Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable from the Microsoft website and install it. This fixes most dependency issues.
